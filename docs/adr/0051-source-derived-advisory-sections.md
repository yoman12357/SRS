# ADR-0051: Select source-linked threat/risk hypotheses and glossary terms

The actual campus draft labelled normal authorized activity as a threat and
proposed automated isolation despite its unconditional exclusion. Its glossary
also repeated a claim of compliance with unspecified auditing standards.

Build applicable defensive planning candidates from recognized source boundaries,
manual-action misuse, recording/retention duties, explicit exclusions and unresolved
decisions. Local section specialists select/order candidate IDs. The coordinator
preserves source links and composes advisory text that introduces no new product
control. Hypotheses are not incidents; control coverage is not proof of enforcement.
Unprovided risk ratings/owners remain unknown/TBD. Mitigations test/review existing
obligations and require scope decisions before adding behavior; they cannot override
an exclusion. Unrecognized threats remain a human-review limitation.

Glossary candidates explain source-present terms using ordinary definitions;
definitions cannot add obligations, fields, technologies or regulations.
Private provenance distinguishes model selections from compiled descriptions.
Status: implementation and real-output verification in progress.
